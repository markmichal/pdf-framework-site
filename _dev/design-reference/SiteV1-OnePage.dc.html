Hi, I'm Bright!
Why it matters

Great tools don't appear fully formed

Like the light bulb, good ministry tools come from curiosity, testing, and teamwork. A shared framework helps our ministry be more fruitful, our teams more aligned, and our products more effective.

Build the right things

Every project starts by listening to the people we serve, so we solve real needs, not hunches.

Waste less

Early, simple testing catches problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Work better together

Shared language helps sponsors, teams, and leaders know where a product is and what it needs next.

Keep products alive

A named Product Lead cares for each tool after Launch, so it doesn't fade when its creator moves on.

The framework at a glance

The Design Cycle
The Deliver Cycle
Immerse
Step into their world
Listen and observe your audience to make sure you're solving the right problem.
Imagine
Dream and decide
Brainstorm creative solutions freely, then pick the most promising idea to test.
Iterate
Test, refine, repeat
Build rough prototypes and use feedback loops to turn inspiration into insight.
Build
Polish and prepare
Finish the product and create training materials to equip your users.
Launch
Share the story
Show the value of the tool and put it into people's hands.
Monitor
Track and maintain
Measure engagement and impact to keep the solution effective over time.
Each stop in detail, in the manual

Primary roles and responsibilities

Sponsor

Starts the project, secures resources, and lines it up with ministry goals.

Project team

Gives creative direction and manages the timeline from Immerse to Launch.

Product lead

Takes over after Launch and leads the long term Monitor phase.
